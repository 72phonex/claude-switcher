Claude Switcher - Feature Ideas
===============================

This list contains practical features that can improve daily use while keeping
the app lightweight, private, and reliable on low-spec laptops.

Desktop and performance
-----------------------
1. Resource monitor
    Show app memory usage, active sessions, and last refresh time.

2. Session recovery
    Detect crashed, blank, or expired sessions and offer a safe reload.

Account workflow
----------------
3. Account search
    Search account names quickly when many accounts are configured.

4. Favorites and account ordering
    Pin important accounts and drag them into a preferred order.

5. Per-account colors and icons
    Make accounts easier to identify without displaying private email data.

6. Account health dashboard
    Show Ready, Limited, Sign-in required, Offline, or Needs attention.

7. Safe settings backup
    Export account names and preferences without exporting cookies or sessions.

8. Account import validation
    Validate imported settings before adding them to the local configuration.

9. Inactive-session cleanup
    Clear cache for unused accounts while preserving login cookies.

Reminders and notifications
---------------------------
10. Configurable reset reminders
    Notify at reset time or 5, 15, and 30 minutes before reset.

11. Per-account notification controls
    Enable or disable reminders independently for each account.

12. Snooze reminders
    Snooze a notification for 10 minutes, 1 hour, or until reset.

13. Reminder history
    Show the last detected limit, reset time, and notification status locally.

Privacy and reliability
-----------------------
14. Encrypted local preferences
    Protect non-session settings using the operating system secure storage.

15. Offline-friendly status
    Show connectivity state and preserve the app shell when Claude is offline.

Keyboard shortcuts
------------------
Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+9   Switch to the account in that sidebar position.
Ctrl+R                  Reload the active Claude session.
Ctrl+Shift+R            Refresh usage for all loaded accounts.
Ctrl+F                  Focus account search.
Ctrl+Shift+M            Toggle low-memory mode.
Ctrl+Shift+T            Show or hide the application window.
Ctrl+,                  Open application settings.
Escape                  Close dialogs and search.

Implementation principles
-------------------------
- Keep Claude cookies and login sessions isolated per account.
- Never store or export passwords, cookies, tokens, or private conversations.
- Keep background work throttled and avoid loading inactive webviews.
- Show errors clearly instead of silently falling back.
- Make every privacy-sensitive action explicit to the user.
